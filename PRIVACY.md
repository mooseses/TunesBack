# TunesBack Privacy Policy

*Effective October 23, 2026*

## Your music is nobody's business but yours.

**TunesBack collects no data.** None. No account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking, no "anonymous usage statistics". There isn't even a TunesBack server for anything to be sent to.

### Everything happens on your computer

TunesBack reads your music library and listening history, works out your stats and draws your Wrapped cards right here. What it remembers (your sources, your listens and your settings) is saved in TunesBack's own folders on this computer, and nowhere else.

### We don't collect anything

Not your library, not your listening history, not how you use the app, not crash reports. Nothing reaches us, because there's nothing for it to reach.

### Online features: you're in charge

TunesBack goes online only when you use a feature that needs to:

- **Last.fm**: to read your scrobbles with the user name (and API key) you give it, and to fetch album covers.
- **ListenBrainz**: to read your listens, with album covers from the Cover Art Archive.
- **GitHub**: to see whether there's a newer TunesBack, once a day and when you press Check for Updates. Nothing about you is sent; turn the daily check off in Settings.

These requests go straight from your computer to those services. Like any website, they can see things such as your IP address and what was asked for, and their own privacy policies decide what happens next. **TunesBack can't control what they collect**, so please read their policies before you connect:

- [Last.fm Privacy Policy](https://www.last.fm/legal/privacy)
- [MetaBrainz Privacy Policy](https://metabrainz.org/privacy) (ListenBrainz and the Cover Art Archive)
- [Spotify Privacy Policy](https://www.spotify.com/legal/privacy-policy/)
- [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) (update checks)

Spotify is different: TunesBack never connects to it, and only reads the download you choose. Spotify's policy covers what it keeps and what it put in that download.

### Sharing is your call

Wrapped cards are saved only where you save them. Once you post them, what happens next is up to you and the place you post them.

### Take it all back

Remove a source on the Services or Library page and TunesBack deletes what it imported from it. To erase everything, quit TunesBack and delete its folders (the Dev space lists where they are).

### If this ever changes

Any change to this policy will be announced in the release notes before it ships. Questions? [Ask on GitHub](https://github.com/mooseses/TunesBack/issues).

*You can also read this policy in the app: on the welcome screen, and in About TunesBack.*
