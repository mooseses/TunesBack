TunesBack collects no data. Your music and listening history stay on your computer.

Everything Wrapped shows you, for the music you own.

Spotify has Wrapped and Apple Music has Replay. If you keep your own library or listen across several apps, TunesBack tells you what your year sounded like.

An Overview you arrange Listening time, plays, your top artist, song and album, genres, decades and more, as tiles you move, resize and choose yourself.
Wrapped cards to share Story or square images in several layouts and nine themes, one tap to shuffle, every card editable.
When you listen By hour and weekday, month by month, your longest streak, busiest day and the artists you discovered.
Listening Age How old your taste is: the era you keep coming back to, explained step by step with your own numbers.
Top charts Songs, albums, artists, genres, release years, new finds and skips, for this year, last year, all time or your own dates.
At home on your computer A native sidebar on macOS, Mica on Windows 11, the global menu on KDE Plasma. Light and dark.

Cards worth posting.

Every card has its own layouts: a list, a spotlight, a receipt, a vinyl record, a calendar. Pick a theme for the deck, or let Shuffle give every card its own. The Cover theme takes its colours from your top album.

1080 × 1920 for stories, 1080 × 1080 for posts. Titles in any language.

How old is your taste?

Your Listening Age finds the five release years you play most and works out how old you'd be if you were 18 in the middle of them. Younger than you are? You're into new music. Older? You know what you like.

It's opt-in: it shows up on your Overview and in your cards only if you want it to.

Your music is nobody's business but yours.

No account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. There isn't even a TunesBack server for anything to be sent to. TunesBack only goes online to read the accounts you connect, fetch album covers for them, and check for updates.
